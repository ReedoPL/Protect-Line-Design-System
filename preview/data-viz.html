Charts that look like us

Eight series colours in a fixed order, led by navy and hot pink; sequential and diverging ramps from the brand ramps; and a check that every neighbouring pair stays distinct for colour-blind readers.

Categorical, in order

Assign slot 1, then 2, then 3 — never skip, never cycle, and keep a series' colour when a filter removes others. Past eight series, fold the tail into “Other”. Dark mode uses the same hues, stepped for navy.

Ratios are against the chart surface. Under 3:1 (amber on white) the marks need a visible label or a table view beside them; coral is only just over. Status colours (success, warning, danger) are never series colours.

As colour-blind readers see it

Slots 1 → 8, simulated (Machado et al. 2009, full severity)Min ΔE00

Min ΔE00 is the smallest CIEDE2000 difference between neighbouring slots, recomputed here for the current theme. The gate in scripts/cvd-report.js is 10; the palette clears 20 in every simulation, light and dark. In scatter plots and maps, where any two colours can touch, stop at three series.

In use

Thin bars with a 4px rounded end, a 2px gap between touching marks, hairline grid. Labels and values use text colours, never the series colour. Hover or tab to a mark for its values; every chart has a table view.

Ramps

Sequential ramps are one hue, light to dark; in dark mode they flip so small values sink into the navy. The diverging ramp runs navy-blue to pink through a neutral midpoint. Every step is an existing brand colour.
