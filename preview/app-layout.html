One frame, every page

A navy sidebar, a page with one title, and a side column that arrives at 1200px. Two breakpoints and a handful of fixed widths; everything else is fluid.

A record's page

The header band carries the record's name, its state and the one group of actions. The tab row sits under it and stays at the top of the window as the page scrolls. From 1200px a 340px side column sits beside the main one; below that it follows it. The page undoes its padding once, and the band, the tab row and the body each add their own back.

Price is the first reason, but not the main one

In 38% of Manchester's cancellations the customer said the policy cost too much. In two thirds of those calls they had also not been told when the first payment would leave their account.

London's customers gave price as the reason less often, and were told the payment date in nine calls out of ten.
