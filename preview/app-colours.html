Surfaces and depth

Depth comes from tone and a hairline border, not shadow. Only floating layers (menus, toasts, dialogs) carry the one overlay shadow. Dark mode steps up the navy ramp — never grey or black.

Accent, focus and boundaries

Hot pink is 4.13:1 on white — fine for a focus ring or an indicator bar (3:1), not for text at app sizes. So fills under white text use pink-600, and pink text uses pink-700.

Status

Five states plus neutral. Each has a tint, a border, text, and a solid. Status always comes with a word and a dot or icon — never colour alone. Danger is a true red, well clear of the brand pinks, so an error never reads as a call to action.
