# Protect Line voice (agent summary)

Distilled from the official tone of voice guide. For the full handbook, see `brand/source/tone-of-voice.md`.

**Investing in Our People to Protect Yours.**

## Writing rules

- **British English** — colour, organise, cheque (no American z's)
- **Sentence case** — only capitalise sentences and proper nouns
- **Verbs over nouns** — "start" not "commence", "get" not "obtain"
- **Left-align** long copy; centre only short headlines
- **Explain jargon once**, then use the term
- **Emojis as spice** — not the main course

## Greetings and sign-offs

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Dear [Name] / Hi [Name] / Hello [Name] | Hey mate / Hiya! / Wassup? |
| All the best / Many thanks / Kind regards | Cheers mate / Peace out! |

## Tone examples

**Good:** "When it comes to whole of life insurance, think of it as a lifelong companion. It's a policy that covers you for your entire life, not just a set period."

**Bad (jargon overload):** "The policyholder, colloquially known as the insured, assumes the financial risk stipulated within an insurance policy."

**Bad (fear-mongering):** "If you neglect life insurance, your family could face insurmountable debts."

**Bad (too vague):** "The insurance buyer takes on financial risk and pays regularly for protection."

## Insurance copy

- Be clear about what cover does without scaring people
- Use standard industry terms (policyholder, premium, beneficiary) with a brief explanation
- Never oversimplify to the point of confusion
- Never use fear tactics or pressure language
